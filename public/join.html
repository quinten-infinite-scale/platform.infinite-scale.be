How you get paid

Three ways to earn

Depending on the role and client, pay is structured in one of these ways — sometimes combined.

Per hour
Hourly rate
Fixed pay for your time on the phone — predictable income regardless of outcome.
Per result
Per appointment
A fixed fee per qualified result delivered. The more you produce, the more you earn.
Commission
% of the deal
A percentage of the deal value when it closes. Higher upside — best for experienced setters.
